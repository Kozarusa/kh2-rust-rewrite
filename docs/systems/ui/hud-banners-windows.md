## Text banners and the experience popup (WP-4B2)

Module `hud_banner.rs`.

- The `fiel` image holds the word strips FINISH!, COMBO!, CHANCE!, PUSH!, OBTAINED MUNNY!, NEXT LEVEL and the munny digits. The groups were matched to words by the texel rectangles each group draws: 66 FINISH!, 62 COMBO!, 58 and 60 CHANCE!, 64 PUSH!, the odd group before each (57, 61, 63, 65) is the short opening animation (a triangle button with a burst), 48 NEXT LEVEL, 17 OBTAINED MUNNY!. Inferred from the sprite rectangles and the pictures; the callers that create these objects (0x151970 with group 0x11 and 0x151d48 with group 0x30, each with six child players of groups 0x1c and 0x26) were not followed, so when each word is shown and where is not known.
- Experience popup (0x15b198 builds, 0x15ae80 lays out, 0x15b010 formats): the root is group 16 (the munny symbol), the digits are groups 6 to 15 (table 0x358c50), the amount is formatted with `%d`. Each digit advances by the `unk20` of its group; the root's offset is half the total width minus the symbol's width and digit `k` is at `sum of earlier advances - half`, so the group is centred on the popup's position. The game places the popup at a projected world point and the counter caps at 9,999,999 (seven digits). The value it shows is the one `FUN_001b51e0` adds to the experience total at 0x340e48 (see docs/systems/combat-effects/prizes.md, correction: that word is the experience, not the munny; the "OBTAINED MUNNY!" strip is raised by the prize pickup 0x1a39b0). Hooked to the enemy defeat and ON by default since WP-EX2 (`KH2_EXP_POPUP=0` turns it and the strip off). Why the digit and prefix came apart (WP-EX2): the digits are NOT children of the symbol. The layout 0x15ae80 stores the symbol's offset at +0x16c (applied by the draw 0x138bf0 on top of the object's screen point +0x164/+0x168) and each digit's offset at +0xabc+4k, and the per-frame draw 0x15b0f0 puts digit k at the object's own point plus that offset (sprite x = +0x164 + offset, y = +0x168). The port had made the digits children of the symbol node, so each also inherited the symbol's offset and the symbol group's anchor; they now hang off no parent and use the popup's point directly. The symbol sits AFTER the digits (offset half minus symbol width = the end of the digit run), as clip 9 shows (a digit, then the small symbol). Projection (0x139358 per frame): the world point at +0x190 is multiplied by the matrix at manager+0x20 (0x13a380: 4x4, perspective divide, integer), then x - 0x700 and y - 0x730 (the GS centre 2048 minus half of 512x448), so the result is in the 512x448 HUD units, the same space as `viewport_to_hud`; the sprite is drawn only while the depth at +0x1a0 is in 1..0x3d0900. The point is the defeated enemy's shape kind 0x11 point (0x16bbe8). `progress::pay_defeats` sends `progress::ExperienceGained { amount, to_next, at }` (world point of the defeated enemy), `hud_banner::update` projects it with the game camera (`hud_gfx::viewport_to_hud`) and adds a timed popup (removed when its group has played out), and `update_strips` adds the timed "NEXT LEVEL" strip (`StripKind::PopupTotal`, group 48) with `to_next`. The strip object (0x151d48, registered by 0x14f8b0 with kind 0xe) never sets a screen point of its own: +0x164/+0x168 are 0 (0x1389b0), so the strip draws where its sequence group puts it, the top-left corner (clip 9: "NEXT LEVEL 4" at the top left, "OBTAINED MUNNY!" under it); the port uses position 0,0 (`PICKUP_STRIP_POS`). WP-HU3 (clip 9 against the port at 1280x720): the strip roots were `Edge::Center`, so on a wide window the 4:3 layout was centred and the strip started about 245 px in; they are now `Edge::Left` (`strip_node`), like the command deck, and the NEXT LEVEL strip sits at about 15..200 px (label text from about 43 px, 3.4% of the width) against clip 9's 18..140 of 720 (label text from 28 px, 3.9%; the clip's picture is a 720x365 band of the 720x486 frame, rows 61..425). Clip 9 frame 4.0 s shows both strips: the label rows are about 9.3% of the band apart (86 and 120 of the frame), the port stacks the munny strip by the NEXT LEVEL group's `unk1c`; that spacing was not compared in a harness run (still [inferred]). Vertical gap: the label sits at 6.6% of the band in the clip and 10% of the frame in the port; the clip's command deck is at 77.5% against the port's 75.4%, so the clip band is not the same crop of the screen and the vertical position was left as the sequence's own. The registry's offset (+0x24 = the sum of the stored `unk1c` of the groups of the lower kinds that are up, 0x14f868) puts the munny strip (kind 0x11) under the NEXT LEVEL strip while it shows [inferred: summed each frame]. Gap: how the strip digits are placed relative to the label (0x151ca0 stores running sums at +0xad0 that include the digit's own width; the draw that applies them was not read), and when the registry entry of a finished strip is cleared (clip 9 suggests when it ends).
- Inputs: `HudBanner(Option<{ kind, pos }>)` and `ExperiencePopups(Vec<{ amount, pos }>)`. `KH2_HUDBANNER=<push|chance|combo|finish|nextlevel|munny>[:x:y]`, `KH2_HUDEXP=<amount>[:x:y]` (older name `KH2_HUDMUNNY` still read), and `KH2_HUDFREEZE=<frame>` to hold their sequences at a frame for a screenshot.

## The INFORMATION bar (WP-I1)

Module `hud_info.rs` (a submodule of `hud_mission.rs`, run from its `update`). The strip at the top left with the
italic word INFORMATION and the mission objective under it (reference clip 3.mp4: "Defeat Seifer!").

- **Who shows it** (verified): the mission start `FUN_001b8ee8` ends with `FUN_001b9a08(0)`: the header's u16 at +6,
  when not 0, is looked up (`FUN_00190c40`) and passed to `FUN_0014f1e8`, which builds the bar object
  `DAT_0035852c` (made by `FUN_0014ef70`, vtable 0x358668) with `FUN_00150428`, or swaps the text of a bar that
  is up (`FUN_001505b0`). `FUN_0014f240(id)` closes it early (`FUN_001507d8`). Mission messages: docs/systems/world-minigames/struggle.md
  "The battle intro" (`TT04_MS500` 0x48c1, `TT04_MS108` 0x375a, `TT03_MS400` 0x48bf; the Struggle `TT04_MS00` has 0).
- **The pieces** (verified): `gaug` (the field file's second pair, `FUN_0013a998(1)`). The text object (+0x30,
  `FUN_00138fc8`: font 0, colour 0xe5e5e5 alpha 0x80, size the group record's +0x18 or 15, flags 4, 8 and 0x10)
  plays group 0 (11 frames: the bar and the INFORMATION plate fade in) with follow-up group 1 (+0x1ac; loops from
  frame 61: the plate's shine); the second object (+0x220, a plain sequence object) plays group 0 with follow-up
  group 2 (+0x39c; a black sprite that slides from x 0 to 360 over frames 20 to 100: it uncovers the text from
  left to right). Both draw tag 7 at priority 8000 (`FUN_00139430`), so an event's field-HUD hide (tags 3 to 6,
  `FUN_0014ef48`) leaves them up; `hud_mission` keeps them out of its hide. Group records: x 6, y -12, +0x1c 45
  (the height for the stacking, `FUN_0014f8b0` row 1 of column 0), +0x20 360 (the scissor width).
- **The update** `FUN_00150688` (verified): the object's +0x188 (set by `FUN_00138aa8` when a follow-up starts)
  raises flag 2; from then the text's scissor is (0, stack y, the group's +0x20, 64) (`FUN_0014b6c0`, before it 1
  x 1), a wait of 180.0 (0x3792b0) runs down by `DAT_00356f44` per update, then the text's x (+0x1c0, the text
  object's +0x190, set to the group record's x by `FUN_00150510`) drops by 1 per update until it is below the
  start x minus the text width (`FUN_001a7ed8`), and `FUN_001507d8` sets group 3 (20 frames: everything fades out,
  no follow-up, so the object ends, `FUN_00138aa8` vtable +0x14) and hides the second object. `FUN_00150510`
  plays system sound 0x22 (`FUN_002c14a8`; not played here, as for the item messages).
- **The text's place** (`FUN_00139048`, verified): the group's anchor (`FUN_00138f38`) plus the group record's x,
  y (flag 0x10) plus the stacking offset (+0x164, +0x168) plus the scrolled x.
- **Port** (`hud_info::update`, `hud_info::step`): as above, with [measured] 0.5 units per 60 Hz tick for the
  scroll (the clip moves the text about 27 capture pixels a second; the 180 wait reads as 3 s at 60 Hz, so the
  update that moves the text by 1 runs every other frame [inferred]). The scissor: the bar is `Edge::Left` (the
  user's widescreen rule), so its left edge, screen x 0, is the window's edge; glyphs left of it are dropped whole
  (the app's text path has no scissor) [approximation]. Tests
  `the_information_text_waits_then_scrolls_out_and_the_bar_closes`,
  `the_seifer_missions_name_their_objective_in_the_header`. Logs `hud: INFORMATION bar: message 0x48c1 "Defeat
  Seifer!" ...`, `hud: INFORMATION bar open`, `... scrolled out, closing (group 3, FUN_001507d8)`, `hud:
  INFORMATION bar gone`. Harness `KH2_INFO=<hex id>` shows any message. Looked at: `local/i1_b45_6.png` (bar,
  plate and text at the top left as in the clip at 1.5 s), `local/i1_compare.png`.
- **Not ported**: the stacking with other row-0 elements of column 0 (none in these missions), `FUN_001505b0`'s
  re-show and `FUN_0014f240`'s early close (their callers were not traced), the sound.

## Damage numbers

The original draws no damage numbers. The only floating-number system found is the digit popup at 0x15b198 (above), called from the enemy-defeat code (0x1b25d0) and 0x1c46a8 with a value that is added to the experience total (0x1b51e0, capped at 9,999,999). So it is an experience gain popup. The old floating damage text remains only as a debug aid behind `KH2_DEBUGHUD=1` or F9.

## HP digits (WP-6D)

The original field HUD draws no HP numbers: not for Sora, the party or a target. Searched:
- Sora's panel (class 0x156520, per-frame 0x157a50, the HP rule 0x155400 to 0x155530, the switch 0x157230) and the party panel (0x154a78, 0x154818, 0x154930) set only gauge groups, angles and masks; none of them formats a number or starts a digit group.
- The target gauge (0x1540a0, 0x154498, 0x154758) draws bars and pips only.
- Every number formatter the HUD code calls: `%d` at 0x379430 through 0x15b010 (experience popup 0x15ae80 and the number strips 0x1518c8 / 0x151ca0, which 0x1a39b0 (the munny pickup, strip group 17) and 0x15b198 (the experience popup, strip group 48 "NEXT LEVEL") feed) and `%09d` at 0x3792a8 through 0x14fed0, which turns the low N digits of a value into digit groups from a table. Its callers: 0x14ff98 (two five-digit counters, digit groups 12 to 21 from 0x3585f0 and 24 to 33 from 0x358618, values read through pointers the creator passes), 0x150d40 (three two-digit fields split by 0x14c7f0, a clock), 0x225df0 (three digits, table 0x36be88), 0x22e300 (six digits). 0x14ff98 belongs to the object built by 0x14f540, which only 0x1cf8d8, 0x1cff20 and 0x1d2e20 create (methods in the tables at 0x360580 to 0x360760); the values come from their own records (+0x2c, +0x18, +0x38), not from the player's HP. These are mission counters and timers [inferred from their layout: two counters with a separator group, a clock]. None of these is called from the gauge panels; the values 0x225df0 and 0x22e300 show (code in the 0x22xxxx range, outside the field HUD) were not traced.
- The `gaug` groups 28 to 37 are the drive-point digits (0x3589c0), used by the drive gauge.

So there is nothing to port; the item is closed. The pause menu's status screen was not searched (outside the field HUD).

## Message windows (WP-6D)

Module `hud_msgwin.rs`; rules in `kh2_image::window` and `Seqd::resize_frame`. Verified in code unless marked.

**Files.** The frame is a sequence: `msg/us/fontinfo.bar`, entry `md_m` (type 25), loaded by 0x1a8ff8 into 0x1c958b0 (returned by 0x1a9dc8). Its texture is the window texture object 0x1c95710 (returned by 0x1a9db8, set up empty by 0x1a9e40): 0x190b70, which binds a message file as table `n`, uploads the file's `md_m` IMGD (type 24) into it through 0x1a9368 when the entry is not empty. The world's message file `msg/us/<world>.bar` is bound as table 2 when an area loads (0x168ba8 calls 0x190e70 with the world byte 0x33e830); the system file `sys.bar` and the special files `jm.bar` and `gumi.bar` carry an empty `md_m`. The world files sit in the world sub-indexes (`000tt.idx`); each holds a 256x128 8-bit image, and the images differ from world to world (tt, hb, eh, al compared). Checked by `window_frames_in_the_font_and_world_files` in `kh2-image/tests/disc.rs`.

**Frame kinds and groups.** The base window 0x1aaf20 takes a frame kind 0 to 9 (its eighth argument, stored at +0x1d0) and plays groups from the string at 0x35ea48, three characters per kind: `kind*3` opening (state 0), `+1` idle (state 1 on, 0x1ab170), `+2` closing (state 5 to 6). The characters are "CDEF..." so kind `k` uses groups 67 + 3k, 68 + 3k, 69 + 3k. In the file the opening and closing groups play once (loops 1) and the idle group loops; each draws one nine-part sprite group (kind 0: 16x16 corners at texels (0,0)-(32,48), the orange rounded frame; kind 4: texels (51,97)-(81,127), the black frame). The opening animation of kind 0 is a black copy at alpha 0x10 to 0x20 sliding to (3, 3) under the frame fading in from alpha 0x40, a drop shadow [inferred from the colours and offsets].

**Frame sizing (0x2387c0).** Every draw (0x1ab370) first rewrites the nine parts of the current group's first animation's sprite group in the shared sequence data: the box is the text size plus the group record's `x` and `y` (margins: kind 0 16 and 8, kind 1 10 and 8, kind 2 16 and 8, kind 3 8 and 8, kinds 4 to 9 none), full size rounded down to even; corners keep their size outside the box, edges are stretched along it, the centre fills it, all centred on the frame's draw point. Test `frames_stretch_around_the_box` and the disc test check the part rectangles.

**Text box.** The window measures its message (0x191570: every page up to an end-of-text 0x10, widest line and sum of line heights plus gaps, the largest page, each side rounded up to even) and keeps the size at +0x5c and +0x60 (text object +0x50, +0x54). The text is drawn by 0x1aac80 through 0x1a80d8 at the window's x, y with the text object's alignment (+0x64, 3 = centred both ways, set by the base constructor 0x1aa928) and the colour of `ftst` id given to the constructor, looked up for the current world (0x1a7df8).

**Classes.** Constructors with their values (font, colour id, x, y, frame kind):
- 0x1ab6a8 (vtable 0x35eb80): font 3, colour 5, (128, 104), kind 0. Opened by the event message code 0x2275a8 for message kind 0, by 0x172360 and by 0x2c5cf0 (which moves it to (256, 48) with alignment 2).
- 0x1ab8e0 (vtable 0x35eac0): font 0 at size 16 (0x1a7f60), colour 6, (256, 52), kind 4; the y is moved down by half the text height so the text's top is at 52; the frame is always 432 wide (0x1ab990 passes 0x1b0). Opened by 0x2275a8 for message kind 1 and by 0x2c5cf0.
- 0x1ab7d8 (vtable 0x35eb00): font 3, colour 4, (256, 32), kind 3, alignment 2 (centred on x, top at y); the frame is drawn half the text height below y (0x1ab4e8). Opened by 0x1a5608.
- 0x1ab9e0 (vtable 0x35eb40): font 3, colour 5, (256, 336), kind 0, with a speech tail (0x1aa628: group 47). Also opened by 0x1aba58, the window the talk task's callback 0x1a5a60 builds for op 1 of a townsperson's talk program (docs/systems/events-movies/npc-savepoints.md section 3, "The task"): same values (font 3, colour 5, (256, 336), kind 0, tail `FUN_001aa628(1, 1, 0)`), read in the decompilation of 0x1a5a60; that 0x1aba58 builds it by calling 0x1ab9e0 rather than a copy of the values is [inferred] (the body of 0x1aba58 was not read for this note). The talk callback registers it (0x1aad78), waits until its state (+0x70) is 3 and closes it through +0x20 (docs/systems/events-movies/npc-savepoints.md section 3). Ported as class 3 of `ScriptWindow`: `npc.rs` sets `ScriptWindow.open = (message id, 3)` and waits for `closed`; the page split at text command 0x10, the box measured over all pages, the tail (group 47) and the closing states are `hud_msgwin.rs`'s (docs/systems/core-runtime/contract.md E3 status).
- 0x1aae08 / 0x1aae60 (vtable 0x35ec00): a frameless text line, font 4, colour 3, (256, 365), alignment 4 (a subtitle line) [inferred]; opened by 0x227478. Not ported.

**Draw positions (0x1ab4e8, 0x1ab990).** The frame is drawn centred on (x, y) (alignment 2: on (x, y + h/2)); the prompt point is (x + w/2, y + h/2) (alignment 2: (x + w/2, y + h)), with w and h the text box, not the frame. The button prompt is group 0x41 there (0x1aa3a0, 0x1aa4e8), started when the page is complete and the text has no selection slots; 0x42 plays when the window closes (0x1aa3f8).

**Port.** `MsgWindow(Option<Request { tokens, class }>)` shows one window: opening group, then the idle group with the text (typewriter as in the text engine) and, once the text is complete, the prompt. `KH2_MSG=<hex id>[:class]` opens one for screenshots (class 0 to 3 = the four constructors above in order). Not ported: closing (states 4 to 7), page breaks (only the first page is drawn), the selection-slot prompt (0x1aa128 / 0x1aa158), the speech tail (0x1aa628 / 0x1aa6b8: group `2*(4a + b) + 0x25` at x + w * {-0.5, -0.15, 0.15, 0.5}[c & 3] from 0x35ea68, y - h/2 for c < 4), the child window of 0x1ab708 / 0x1abad0, the window list order (0x1aa928 sorts windows by a priority), and who opens windows in play (the event and script code that calls the constructors is not ported).

## Tutorial windows (WP-T1)

Module `hud_tutorial.rs` (a child of `hud_msgwin.rs`); the footage is video 2.mp4 (the Roxas tutorial in the Sandlot). Verified in code or data unless marked.

**What the footage shows** (frames read to 1/60 s, content area 4:3 of the remaster's 16:9): the scene fades in from black in 0.3 s; the black translucent box (top centre, about 67 % of the picture width, top edge at 9 % of its height, no orange frame) is there at 0.1 s and its text at 0.3 s; the keyblade-shaped prompt sits under its bottom-right corner from the first full frame; a yellow down arrow fades in at 0.45 s over the HP ring, bobs about 25 px of 900 (period about 63 ticks, brighter at the top) and, in the footage's second page, stands above the MP bar; pages change instantly (3 lines: HP gauge; 2 lines: MP gauge; 3 lines: yellow mark; 1 cyan line: "Approach and attack Seifer."); the box is gone at 8.4 s. The command deck (only "Attack") and the HP ring are the normal field HUD (the footage's remaster draws them larger).

**The data.**
- The text is in `msg/us/tt.bar`: 0x3ef7 "The gauge in the bottom right is the HP gauge. You lose HP when damage is taken, and the battle is over when HP reaches 0." (same words as the footage's first page) and 0x4309 (the HP sentence, "When you approach a target, an orange mark will appear. Press [button] to attack." and, after a colour command 0x07 (0, 240, 240, 128) = cyan, "Approach and attack Seifer."). The footage's yellow mark and cyan pages are the two halves of 0x4309; its MP page (blue gauge, "mysterious power") is not on the PS2 disc in any table searched (`sys.bar`, `tt.bar`, `jm.bar`): remaster text [GAP]. The USA disc also holds 0x4306 to 0x430b (armor shop, accessory shop, camera lock, save: the other objective-style tutorials) and 0x3ece to 0x3f04 (older wording).
- The opener found: `msn/us/TT04_MS107.bar` (Seifer, `N_EX570_BTL` parameter 1, docs/systems/world-minigames/struggle.md; header counter max 3): its event 3 thread starts with `push 0x3ef7` and host call 1.296 (table 0x35bce8 group 1 index 296 -> `FUN_001744a0`), then binds object 0x678 (Seifer), `host 1.84` (gauges) and `1.231` (his lowest HP 1). Its event 10 with argument 123 (an area trigger) waits 12 (units of `frame_time`) and sends 0x3ef9 through host 1.284 (`FUN_00174430`: the page sequence object of the 0x2f46xx family, class vtable 0x35c8d8, which holds a 0x1ab8e0 window and raises HUD flag bit 1) [not ported]. The other scripts that push these ids: TT02_MS201 (0x3efd, 0x3efe), TT14_MS110 (0x3ef3), TT32_MS302 / 303 (0x3f04, 0x3f02). Searched without a hit for 0x4306 to 0x430b: every `msn/us/*.bar`, `ard/*.ard` first BDX, `00common.bdx` (their opener is a GAP; objects' own BDX were not scanned).
- The window: host 1.296 builds class `FUN_001abba0` (alloc 0x1194; constructor `FUN_001ab830` over the base 0x1aaf20: x 256, y 0x34, frame kind 4, font 0 at 16 = class 1 of `window::CLASSES`) and registers it (`FUN_001aad78`). Its constructor sets `DAT_00356f1c` bit 8 (`FUN_0014c748(8)`) and byte 0x35ea48[0x1e]; the destructor `FUN_001abce8` clears them (`FUN_0014c760(8)`, `FUN_001befc0(0xc)`). The word's bit 1 is the pause menu (`FUN_0014a080`), bit 4 the events (`FUN_00195ae0`); `FUN_0014c7b8` counts play time only while it is 0 [what bit 8 does beyond that: GAP; the list record 0xc of `FUN_001bef48` is a row of the pause-menu record table `DAT_01d5df08` (WP-E6b, docs/systems/events-movies/event-player.md section 6), whose effect is a GAP]. A window with one page and no child shows its prompt and closes on logical pad bits 0x40c (`FUN_001ab170` case 2 -> `FUN_001aa430`, sound 0x18, close `FUN_001aacd8`: confirm 0x4, cancel 0x8, 0x400 unknown).
- The pointer arrow. Message 0x3ef7 starts with the text command 0x0f and five bytes `01 c7 01 36 01` = (kind 1, x 455, y 310). The text layout (`FUN_001a8210` case 0xf) calls the owner window's method +0x2c with them; the window's vtable slot is `FUN_00332720` -> `FUN_001aa858(window + 0xb0c, kind, x, y)`, which stores x and y and, when kind is not 0, starts group `kind + 1` of the window sequence (`md_m` in `fontinfo.bar`, texture `md_m` of the world message file) on the pointer's own player. Every window draw (vtable slot 4 = `FUN_001ab990`) runs the window draw `FUN_001ab370` and then the pointer's (`FUN_001aa8c0`: step, position, draw). `md_m` groups 2 to 9 are eight arrow variants (one part of 45x47 texels at (210, 1) of the picture, a grey arrow the sequence tints; group 2 = kind 1 is the down arrow of the footage). Message 0x3f02 has `03 64 00 6d 01` (kind 3 at (100, 365), the left deck: "The Items command has been added"), 0x3ef9 and 0x4309 have no 0x0f. Test `the_tutorial_messages_on_the_disc`.

**Port.** `hud_msgwin::pointer_of` reads the command and `hud_msgwin::update` shows the pointer node when the text starts (anchored to the right edge for x above 256, else the left edge [inferred, like the ring and the deck]); `hud_tutorial.rs` opens the windows:
- `struggle::MissionStart` of `TT04_MS107` opens 0x3ef7 (the script's first action); J or K closes it (the game's confirm / cancel) [the keys are inferred]. The follow-up 0x3ef9 (host 1.284) is not ported.
- `KH2_TUTORIAL=1` (optionally `KH2_TUTORIAL_AT=<app second>`) replays the footage on any area: 0x3ef7 at 0.10 s, 0x4309 at 5.50 s, closed at 8.40 s (`hud_tutorial::TIMELINE`, test `the_timeline_follows_the_footage`). `KH2_SCREENSHOT=<png> KH2_SHOT_AT=<s>` as usual: with `KH2_TUTORIAL_AT=6` a shot at 7.6 s is the footage's 1.5 s.
- Looked at (not in the repo): `local/t1_compare_hp.png`, `local/t1_compare_objective.png` (footage frame left, port right): box, text, the keyblade prompt and the yellow arrow match in place and size; the port's party rings (Sora's party) lie under the arrow where the footage has Roxas alone.

**Not ported / differences.** The footage's bobbing amplitude and the arrow's fade-in are the sequence group's own animation (not measured against the group); the window's closing (states 4 to 7) is not ported (the window disappears at once, the footage's box vanishes within 0.1 s); the footage's MP page and the second arrow (over the MP bar) have no PS2 message or 0x0f command; the opener of 0x4309; HUD flag bit 8 and the list record 0xc; the 12-unit wait and 0x3ef9 (host 1.284); the typewriter (the footage's remaster shows whole pages at once, the port types as the PS2 text engine does).
